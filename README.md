### `pricila@github ~ $ cat principles.md`

- **Contracts before code** — endpoints, payloads and error states are defined before implementation.
- **Integrations are the product** — a reliable ERP flow beats a clever endpoint.
- **Readable over clever** — the next reader is usually future me.
- **Document the why** — decisions and gotchas live next to the code.
- **Test what can break** — business rules deserve tests; everything else deserves attention.
